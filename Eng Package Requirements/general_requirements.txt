
### Functional Requirements
============================
->These use the 9-part template: Actor, Condition, Action, Object, Constraints, Object Refinement, Source, Destination, Action Refinement.

1. The hoisting system, upon activation of the lift command, shall lift the steel plate at a variable speed not exceeding 0.5 m/s for plates weighing up to 2000 kg, from the ground level to a maximum clearance height of 5 m while maintaining a strictly horizontal orientation.
2. The magnetic attachment module, upon receiving the engage signal, shall generate a magnetic holding force of at least 1.5 times the weight of the payload for any steel plate handled, from the magnetic contact face across the entire mating area within 2 seconds of signal receipt.
3. The hoisting system trolley, during lateral transport operations, shall transport the magnetically attached steel plate without exceeding a lateral acceleration that causes a pendulum sway greater than 5 degrees for varying payloads up to 2000 kg, from the loading zone to the designated unloading coordinate while maintaining continuous magnetic engagement.
4. The magnetic attachment module, upon receipt of the release command coupled with a weight-on-ground confirmation, shall release the steel plate by reducing residual magnetism to less than 1% for the previously secured plate, from the magnetic contact face to a fully detached physical state within 3 seconds.
5. The hoisting control system, upon activation of the emergency stop switch, shall halt all mechanical movement completely within a maximum of 1.5 seconds for both the hoist line and trolley, from its current operational speed to a completely stationary state without interrupting power to the magnetic holding module.

# Non-Functional Requirements
==============================
->These use the 3-part template: System/Entity, Quality to be achieved, Description.

6. The hoisting system's electronic control enclosure must possess a minimum IP54 protection rating to prevent dust and water ingress within an environment open to the elements.
7. The hoisting system's mechanical braking unit must default to a fully engaged, locked state to prevent the payload from dropping in the event of a total facility power loss.
