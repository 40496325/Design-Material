J6 Analog Front-End (ADS1220 — 4 Single-Ended Channels) SPI2: GPIO 11/12/13, CS 14, DRDY 15

Provides AIN0, AIN1, AIN2, AIN3 (J6.5–J6.8) + +3.3VP excitation (J6.1). EERI (motor driver) current sense (1× AIN) + EERI (magnet) current sense (1× AIN) + MCTR (Gearbox) differential load cell (2× AIN) = 4 / 4 channels used (using ADS1220 avoids needing 2 digital GPIOs for an HX711).

J3 / J4 I2C Expander (MCP23017 @ 0x20 — 15+1 Pins) I2C: GPIO 8 (SDA) / GPIO 9 (SCL)

Provides GPA0–GPA7 (J3.3–10) and GPB1–GPB7 (J4.4–10, plus GPB0 via solder jumper JP4). Ideal for slow digital signals (MCTR (Gearbox) hoist brake, slow fault/enable lines, and 5-pin expansion) when J7 direct GPIOs are full. ⚠ Avoid Pin 2 (+5V).
