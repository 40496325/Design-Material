2. What to ask each group member (firmware only)

MCTR 1, AR Cronje (by Wed 18 Nov)

Is the encoder output push-pull 5 V or open collector? Which input circuit is fitted?
HW-095: is ENB jumpered to ENA? Are IN3/IN4 wired to J4.6/J4.7, or jumpered to IN1/IN2?
With IN1 HIGH and IN2 LOW, which end does the trolley move towards?
Can we count 10 wheel turns together? The ICD gives 1050 counts per wheel turn; their note implies about 467.
What is the usable rail length? If they don’t know, we measure it on 24 Nov.

MCTR 2, VA King (by Thu 19 Nov)

Spool radius and total gear ratio.
Hoist motor poles and rated speed at 50 Hz.
Brake on J4.8: is it wired, does HIGH release it, and is a 50 ms lead before the drive starts enough?
Load-cell rated capacity, and 2–3 known masses for 25 Nov.
Is the stroke still 1.6 m? Is there a top or bottom limit switch the MCU should read?

EEII 1, MM Van Rooyen (by Fri 20 Nov)

Is 10 kHz confirmed as the carrier for all three phases?
Does the 4N25 opto stage invert the signal? What minimum and maximum duty does the board need?
What does DRV_EN drive (the IR2104 /SD pins?), and should it be LOW at boot?
DRV_FAULT on J3.5: does it exist, is it active HIGH or LOW, and does it latch?
Current signal: their scope of work says analog below 2.048 V on the shared ADC, but on 29 Oct they said digital. Which is it, on which pin, and what scale?
Start frequency, maximum frequency, V/f and ramp rate.
May the firmware swap phases B and C while running, or must it stop first?
When is the board signed off, and is Thu 26 Nov OK for the joint hook-up?

EEII 2, F Prinsloo (by Thu 19 Nov)

Current sensor on J6.8: volts per amp and the output at 0 A. It must stay at or below 3.3 V at 7.5 A.
Holding threshold. I’ve put 6.0 A as the default if they don’t answer.
MAG_LOGIC on J3.6: an enable, a status signal back, or unused?
Can their switch handle 20 kHz at 8.33 % duty (4.2 µs pulses)? 15khz switches
